Purposes and Legal Basis

For hosting this website, I use GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. (part of Microsoft). My legitimate interest in doing so lies in the secure, globally accessible and cost-efficient operation of this website. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Processing by GitHub

When the website is accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary data (e.g. IP address, browser information, timestamp) as an independent controller, in order to to provide the website, ensure its security, and protect against misuse. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Further details on the categories of data processed, retention periods, recipients and international data transfers can be found in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data Subject Rights

I do not process any personal data in connection with this website myself. Processing is carried out exclusively by GitHub. You can exercise your rights under the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection) directly with GitHub.

Right to Lodge a Complaint
